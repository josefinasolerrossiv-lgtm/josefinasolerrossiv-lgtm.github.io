AntoxMedia

AI-Driven Marketing Agency

Role Social Media Manager & Content Editor
Type Part-Time · Remote
Clients Budare & Mokambo
The Scope

One agency,
two brand voices.

AntoxMedia is an AI-driven marketing agency managing social media and content for multiple client brands. As Social Media Manager & Content Editor, I run the day-to-day Instagram and TikTok presence for two very different concepts: Budare, a Venezuelan food truck, and Mokambo, a brunch restaurant in Seville.

The role covers the full content pipeline — editing short-form video, designing event posters with AI and Canva, brainstorming campaign ideas, and replying to every comment and DM in a tone that stays true to each brand.

The Brands
