LYNA

Menstrual Cycle & Nutrition App

Role AI Content Creator Intern
Duration 2 months
Focus AI Character & Brand Design
The Scope

Giving a wellness app
a face and a voice.

LYNA is an app focused on menstrual cycle tracking and nutrition, designed to help users understand their bodies and build healthier habits. As AI Content Creator Intern, I was brought on to help define the brand's most human-facing element: an AI character that could represent LYNA across its content and community.

I designed LYNA's new brand image as an AI character, researching which visual and personality features best suited the brand, then produced test videos using Google Flow to evaluate the reliability and consistency of the AI-generated character.
