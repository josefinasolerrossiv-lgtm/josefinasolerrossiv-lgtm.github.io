Het
Haartheater

Where Style Becomes Art

Social Media Manager Intern · Apr — Nov 2024 · 5 Salons · Amsterdam
The Challenge

A brand with presence.
Without a voice.

When I joined Het Haartheater, Amsterdam's five-salon luxury hair collective had something extraordinary to offer — skilled artisans, a discerning clientele, and an aesthetic that set them apart. What they lacked was a digital presence that matched their physical elegance.

But the stakes were higher than follower counts. The Netherlands was facing a nationwide shortage of hairdressers and stylists — making it exceptionally difficult for salons to attract qualified professionals. Het Haartheater didn't just need to win clients. They needed to win talent.

The strategy I helped shape went beyond marketing: it turned the studio's unique working attitude, creative culture, and atmosphere into a recruitment tool. Every piece of content was designed to make a potential stylist feel what it would be like to work there — to make the identity of the place irresistible not just to customers, but to the people who could bring it to life.

Scope of Work

What I Did

01

Instagram Growth Strategy

Designed and executed a comprehensive Instagram growth strategy — from content pillars and visual brand guidelines to posting schedules and hashtag architecture tailored for luxury audiences.

02

Client Communication Management

Managed all inbound digital communications — responding to enquiries, maintaining the brand's refined tone of voice across every touchpoint, and building lasting client relationships through social channels.

03

Marketing Plans & Mood Boards

Developed seasonal marketing calendars and visual mood boards that aligned each campaign with Haartheater's luxury positioning — drawing on editorial beauty references to guide art direction and content creation.

04

Advanced Excel Data Tracking

Built custom Excel models tracking revenue and profit margins across all five salons — enabling data-driven decisions on marketing investment, peak seasons, and campaign ROI at both individual salon and group level.

05

Events & Internal Initiatives

Coordinated corporate events and internal team initiatives — from salon-launch communications to seasonal client appreciation moments — ensuring brand consistency from digital to in-person experience.

Featured Achievement

Website Contribution

Beyond social media, I contributed directly to the creation of Het Haartheater's new website — working on the digital presence that would become the brand's primary online touchpoint and anchor its luxury identity across the web.
