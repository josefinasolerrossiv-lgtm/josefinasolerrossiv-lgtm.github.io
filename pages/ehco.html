E H C O EHCO

Connecting the Hispanic community at Erasmus

Social Media Manager & Content Editor

The Mission

Community is
the strategy

EHCO exists to unite and amplify the Hispanic student community at one of Europe's most international universities. As Social Media Manager, my role was to translate the energy, diversity, and pride of hundreds of Hispanic students into digital content that resonated, inspired, and grew.

This wasn't just content strategy — it was cultural storytelling. Every post, story, and campaign needed to feel authentic to a community that spans dozens of countries, languages, and traditions while sharing a common thread: pride in their roots.
