International Hair Brand · Rotterdam

Role Social Media & Marketing Intern
Duration 9 months
Scope Multi-channel Marketing
The Scope

A global brand.
A national strategy.

Toni&Guy is one of the world's most recognised premium hair brands — a name synonymous with fashion-forward styling and professional excellence. As Social Media & Marketing Intern for the Rotterdam division, I was responsible for translating that global brand equity into a locally resonant digital strategy.

I built and managed the multi-channel marketing framework that kept Toni&Guy NL visible, relevant, and growing — across Instagram, LinkedIn, and beyond — for nine months.
